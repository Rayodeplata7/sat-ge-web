Core Architecture

Sensing the invisible dynamics of nature.

Engineered through strict transducer datasheets to eliminate noise, drift, and ambient hysteresis in harsh open-air environments.

Piezoresistive Pressure Array

Powered by Tekscan FlexiForce A201 sensor elements calibrated with 10kΩ pull-down circuits. Measures active lateral load and ground pressure up to 4.4 Newtons with sub-millisecond fidelity.

Optical PSD Displacement

Continuous position-sensitive infrared triangulation (850nm) tracking structural slope movement between 0.0 and 5.0 cm. Fitted with adaptive ambient photon baseline filters.
